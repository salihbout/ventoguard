## Design Context

### Surface type
Databricks App: React (Vite, Du Bois) front end served by a FastAPI back end.

### Audience
Customer / field demo: reliability lead, dispatcher and asset manager at an onshore wind operator.

### Theme
light

### Use case
The reliability lead turns 14-day failure risk into planned work orders, and the asset manager asks the fleet questions in plain language.

### Brand (locked)
- Palette: Lava 600 #FF3621 (10% accent: CRITICAL risk + one primary action per screen), Navy 900 #0B2026, Light #F9F7F4
- Typography: DM Sans 400/500/700 + DM Mono (IDs, SQL, model names)
- Components: Du Bois first (`@databricks/design-system`: Button, Alert, Spinner, DesignSystemProvider); custom table/badges match Du Bois radius/focus conventions
- Voice: Databricks editorial-plain (no superlatives, plain statements, sentence case)

### Additional constraints
- AI output trust: Genie answers always show generated SQL; AI briefs show model + grounding sources.
- Fleet map: Leaflet with OpenStreetMap tiles in greyscale, so the risk colours carry the meaning. The browser fetches the tiles; if they are blocked, the turbines still render on a plain background.
- Customer brand: the Aerion Windkraft AG logo (fictional) sits in the app header, before the VentoGuard wordmark.
